Questions

Good to know.

Do I need a big following?

No. Small, engaged audiences often convert better than big ones. If your followers trust you about trading, apply.

Do I have to post a set number of times?

No quotas and no scripts. Share FxLens when it fits your content. We can send ideas and assets if you want them.

How do I see what I've earned?

Your code's signups, payments and earnings show live inside the FxLens app.

What do my followers get?

A discount on their first month when they use your code. We agree the exact discount with you.

What can't I say?

No guaranteed profits, no "get rich" claims and no fake results. Trading carries risk, and good creators say so.
