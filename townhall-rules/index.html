Townhall Rules

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Townhall is where FxLens traders share takes, wins, and setups. It only works if it stays safe — so every member accepts these rules before joining the conversation, and our moderators enforce them.

01

No adult or explicit content

No pornography or sexually explicit material of any kind — in posts, images, comments, profile photos, or links. Zero tolerance: immediate removal and a ban.

02

No scams, "account management", or money requests

Never ask other members to send you money, crypto, or deposits — and never send yours. "I'll trade your account", "double your money", paid VIP signal groups, and recovery-agent offers are scams. Anything arranged outside the app is entirely at your own risk, and FxLens is not part of it.

03

No broker or prop-firm promotions

Don't post referral or affiliate links for brokers, prop firms, or any financial service, and don't recruit members to outside platforms or groups. FxLens's own recommended brokers are the only broker references on the platform.

04

Not financial advice

Posts in Townhall are personal opinions and experiences from traders, not financial advice. Nobody here can promise profits. Do your own analysis, and never risk money you can't afford to lose.

05

Respect every trader

No harassment, insults, hate speech, threats, or discrimination. Disagree with the trade, not the trader.

06

No impersonation

Don't pretend to be a FxLens mentor, admin, or another trader. Staff will never DM you asking for money, passwords, or deposits.

07

Post real, post honest

No fake profit screenshots, manipulated results, or misleading claims like guaranteed returns. No spam, flooding, or repetitive promotion.

08

Protect your privacy

Don't share your own — or anyone else's — personal details: phone numbers, account numbers, addresses, or documents.

09

Report what breaks the rules

Every post and comment has an anonymous report option in the app. Our moderators review new members' posts before they go live and act on every report. Every post removed for breaking these rules is a strike on your account. 3 strikes in a row means a 72-hour posting ban, and repeat strikes can lead to a permanent ban.

10

Member content is member responsibility

Townhall is member-generated content. We moderate in good faith, but we cannot pre-screen everything, and FxLens is not responsible for what members post or for any losses that result from acting on it. If something looks wrong, report it and we'll act.

How this fits with our Terms

These rules supplement the FxLens Terms of Service (see "Community rules") and our Privacy Policy. Accepting the rules in the app is your agreement to follow them. Questions or appeals: fxlenstechnologies@gmail.com.
